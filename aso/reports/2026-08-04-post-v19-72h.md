## Verdicts on the two open questions

**1. US indexing — RESOLVED: indexed, but not ranking.** The subtitle tokens
were ingested (all combination queries rank, several top-10). Apple simply
scores us below the top-200 cutoff on the head terms themselves, where ranking
is relevance × download/conversion performance. Notably `gratitude jar`
(Astro popularity 17, difficulty 7 — the softest of the three) is still absent,
which says our performance signal on gratitude-family queries is near zero,
not that the terms are too hard in principle.

`gratitude journal` #40→#38→#38 (vs #27 pre-1.9), `manifestation`
