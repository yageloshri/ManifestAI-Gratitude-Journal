across five different keywords.

**What is left is account-level and not visible through the API:**

1. **Billing / payment method not validated.** A PAYG org with an unverified or declined
   card shows campaigns as `RUNNING` while Apple withholds delivery. This is the leading
   hypothesis and it can only be seen in the Apple Ads web dashboard (a red banner on the
   account or Billing page).
2. `sapinLawResponse: NOT_ANSWERED` on the campaign. This is the French advertising-law
   declaration; it should not gate a US-only campaign, and campaign creation succeeded
   without it — but it is the only other unanswered flag on the object.
