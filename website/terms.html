Free features and membership

In version 3, one complete active journey, including steps, outcomes and weekly reviews, is free. Complete a chapter to begin another. Membership allows you to create multiple active journeys at once. Basic writing, reading saved content, export and privacy controls do not require membership.

After membership ends, saved writing and journey history remain readable and exportable. A membership is not a guarantee of cloud backup, AI generation or features not described in the current offer.

Store purchases, renewal and cancellation

Your purchasing store displays the current price, billing period and any eligible introductory offer before you confirm. Prices and available plans can vary by country and store. A free trial applies only if it is explicitly offered to you; there is no universal three-day trial promise for version 3.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through the store where you purchased. For Apple subscriptions, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. For Google Play subscriptions, cancel before the next renewal through Google Play subscription settings. You can normally use the subscription for the period already paid after cancellation, subject to the store’s applicable rules. Deleting the app or erasing local content does not cancel a subscription.

Manage billing and refund requests through the purchasing store. See Apple’s cancellation guidance and Google Play’s cancellation guidance. Restore purchases uses your store account; restoring a membership does not restore a locally deleted journal. The current browser experience does not offer checkout.

Local storage and exports

Version 3 stores writing, journey records and selected photos locally on your device or browser. It does not provide cloud journal synchronization. Keep exports and separate photo copies when needed: uninstalling the app or clearing storage may remove local content. Device backups and exported copies are controlled separately by you and their providers.

When recovery is needed, version 3 can preserve the original local data before restoring a validated backup or creating a new space. Settings can export the latest preserved copy. Erase my space removes version 3 content, its copied vision photos and its preserved new-space recovery archives, but not separately preserved original iOS recovery files. It does not remove copies you exported elsewhere or cancel subscriptions. The privacy policy explains these distinctions.
